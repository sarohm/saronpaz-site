As part of the DareGround project i created a series of toys that challenge the so called innocence of children. Through these toys kids can explore their malicious nature and play, break, tear and poke.

The Wear&Tear doll is created through tearing it apart and making it your own.

The HitTheRoad toy car is made of porcelain so the user can chose how to play with it and the consequences of his actions.

The DoDoDoll can be characterized by pining paper body parts to it.

All MisChief toy have been tested and proved to be loved by kids of all ages.
