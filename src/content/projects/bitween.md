BitWeen is a series of interactive game installation inspired by the classic [pong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pong) game. In our different versions we take a more experience based approach.

**LightPong** 

The LightPong is activated by light and drives a ball of lights on a parabolic trajectory. As you go on the speed of the ball increases.

**SonicPong** 

The SonicPong still plays a game of catch but with sound. As you shout into the racket the sound travels through 30 speakers to the other player and again grows faster with time.

BitWeen is permanently exhibited at the [MadaTech](http://www.madatech.org.il/en) – the Israel National Museum of Science Technology & Space.
