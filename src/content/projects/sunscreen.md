SunScreen is a public digital natural installation that lights up our day! During the first (and only) ungeekcon makeathon we got a chance to tediously put together 144 servo motors and create the first sunlight pixel screen ever!

SunScreen is an attempt to put to good use one of nature’s brightest assets – the sun! During the period of an intense 3 day makeathon we constructed a 144 tile screen and orchestrated them to a few cool applications. SunScreen is a platform on which many more ideas can be implemented.

We created a digital sun dial, a sun message board and even an all time classic snake game.

We hope to have the chance to create a larger scale SunScreen sometime somewhere.

The SunScreen was created in collaboration with MakeLab Israel and our beloved Avi Ostfeld!
