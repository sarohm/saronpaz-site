BoomBox is an accumulative musical instrument. Users are invited to put together an electric drumstick made of an electromagnet, a hammer and a drum made of PVC pipe and a lid made of a material of their choice.

After users finish building their drum stick they can add it to the BoomBox and add their beat to the orchestra. 

Musicians are invited to compose their musical beats especially for the BoomBox orchestra.
