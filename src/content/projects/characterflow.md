CharacterFlow was made for "Exit Strategies", an outdoor art event in Tel Aviv-Yafo in May 2020. As the city came out of the first COVID-19 lockdown and its streets were still empty, the municipality and the Rabinovich Foundation for the Arts invited local artists to create works close to their own homes, where neighbours could come across them on a walk around the block.

I set up a drawing machine, a wall-hung pen plotter, by my home and used it to draw portraits of my neighbours, line by line, out in the open where passers-by could stop and watch the faces slowly appear.
