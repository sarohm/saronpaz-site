Jerusalem Mini Maker Faire is a two-day festival of creativity, invention, design, technology and music at the Science Museum in Jerusalem, part of the international Maker Faire series and the only one of its kind in Israel.

Dozens of makers, artists and inventors from all over the country take over the museum and show their projects to children, young people and families: interactive works, workshops, performances and machines that are sometimes useful and sometimes gloriously useless.

In 2016 the highlight was "Something Does Something", a five-metre chain-reaction machine inspired by Rube Goldberg, designed and built together with the museum's workshop and its visitors, and set off three times a day during the fair.
