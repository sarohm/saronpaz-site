Tired of the meeting before yours running over and hogging the room? TimeLine lights up the meeting-room door frame as the meeting progresses, so everyone inside and outside can see how much time is left.

When time is up, it plays an AI-generated "end of meeting song" to encourage everyone to leave on time.
