The ActionLaser is a brand new product that takes the tech we have developed for the [ActionPainter](../actionpainter/) and brings it to a whole different world. The ActionLaser is based on a laser spirograph that can be controlled by a smartphone. Here is us taking it for a spin.

The ActionLaser was exhibited at the Jerusalem Science Museum makers fair 2013.

The ActionLaser was also featured in a live TV show on the Israeli Channel 10.
