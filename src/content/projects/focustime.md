Need to focus in an open space full of interruptions? FocusTime is a smart-office IoT device that grips the top of your monitor and shows everyone around you, clearly, how much focus time you still need.

It syncs with your calendar, so it can flag virtual interruptions as well as the ones walking up to your desk.
