GrandGesture is a touchless interface for exploring 3D models of real places on a large screen. Standing in front of the screen, a person pans, zooms, rotates and moves up and down through the model with simple hand gestures, picked up by a camera.

The first version was commissioned by vHive, whose drones turn sites such as communication towers into detailed 3D models, so that teams could fly through those models with their hands.

monday.com then commissioned a second version, mGlobe: a digital globe on a large screen, navigated with hand gestures, presenting the work of the company's Emergency Response Team.
