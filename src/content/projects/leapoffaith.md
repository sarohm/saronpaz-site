LeapOfFaith is a test of ones true engagement to another. The ring is designed to resemble a classic diamond ring with one twist – the diamond is completely hidden from sight and covered by a golden crust.

As its name the LeapOfFaith is a testament to the saying ‘seeing is not believing’ and a brave commitment to true love.

The LeapOfFaith was made especially to celebrate my engagement to Dana, the love of my life.
