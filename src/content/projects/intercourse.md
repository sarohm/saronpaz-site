‘The plate, representing private or public space, is part of the table ritual reflecting aesthetic and cultural values both individual and communal. The ritual is meant not only to satisfy our physiological needs, but reflect social custom and traditions. The series of plates exhibited here presents a multidimensional experience through the utilization of daily situations, casting them in a new order that fluctuates between the private and the public’.

DAVID KNAFO, CURATOR.
