‘We know the price of everything but the value of nothing.’

—Oscar Wild

a series of household items up-cycled out discarded products that have been produced but never sold, reversing the production process from the unworthy many to the desirable few.

focusing on an alternative production process; organic like textures are constructed differently each time, thus offering a more expressive working experience replacing a classic industrial assembly line.

Depending on a limited amount of available materials we create collections, changing by the constant flow of available materials.
