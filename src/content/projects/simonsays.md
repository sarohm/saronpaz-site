SimonSays is a spacial recreation of the classic [SIMON](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_(game)) electronic memory game. As a part of the Intel Urban Experience initiative we decided to stretch the game over an entire field, identify layer moves using camera detection technology and by both change the whole game dynamic and turn it into a group, physical, spread out and cooperative game.

SimonSays can also become a platform for future game mechanism development, a technological user insight testing ground and a great place making opportunity.
