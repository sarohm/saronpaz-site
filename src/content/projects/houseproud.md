Inspired by the dutch window decorations I sought to find designers and families to match and redesign their street facing window.

Rethinking the window as a threshold between the private and the public gave both the designers and families the opportunity to explore new grounds and rethink their role the process and towards each other.

The outcome became an urban exhibition for people to roam through and review their surroundings.

An exhibition documenting the process was presented in a local art space and was also used as a meeting point for visitors and participants.
