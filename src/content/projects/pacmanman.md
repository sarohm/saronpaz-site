PacManMan is an experiment in tangible experiential gaming interfaces. Following the current trend of making vistual games physical sought of a way to make the transition a fresh experience of the game that would add a 3rd and 4rth dimension to the virtual existing interface.

Taking the most classic of games – [Pac Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pac-Man) and taking it to the next level. So we made a Pac Man headset which actually turns the player into the Pac Man !

In order to progress in the game you actually need to physically eat the pac man balls and move your mouth to move forward. To turn you tilt your head in the wished direction and continue chewing on.
