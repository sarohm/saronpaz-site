Our work environment shapes our everyday experience, productivity and well-being. mondayMakers is the makers team at monday.com: a team of multi-talented, multi-skilled creators who turn workspaces into dynamic, human-centred environments that foster creativity, focus and collaboration.

We blend the physical and digital realms to create smart-office devices that translate company culture into real-life experiences and real-time data, solving everyday work gripes one at a time.

![mondayMakers](/images/mondaymakers/collage.webp)

### FocusTime

![FocusTime](/images/mondaymakers/focustime-card.webp)

Need to focus in an open space full of interruptions? FocusTime grips the top of your monitor and shows everyone around you, clearly, how much focus time you still need. It syncs with your calendar, so it can flag virtual interruptions as well.

### LightHouse

![LightHouse](/images/mondaymakers/lighthouse-card.webp)

Deep in your work and afraid you'll miss it if something urgent happens? LightHouse is a smart IoT beacon that uses light and sound to alert everyone around it to extreme situations, and syncs with monday.com events. It comes in red and in green.

### TimeLine

![TimeLine](/images/mondaymakers/timeline-card.webp)

Tired of the meeting before yours running over and hogging the room? TimeLine lights up the meeting-room door frame as the meeting progresses, and when time is up it plays an AI-generated "end of meeting song" to encourage everyone to leave on time.

### RoboGong

![RoboGong](/images/mondaymakers/robogong.webp)

A robotic gong connected to monday CRM, bringing the sales team's moments out of the screen and into the office, where everyone can hear them.
