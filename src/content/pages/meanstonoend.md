DESIGN AS A TOOL FOR MEANINGFUL USER EXPERIENCE

Through following the complex relations between design, use and meaning the book presents experience design as an alternative and relevant paradigm for today’s designers to explore.

Throughout the book different experiments are presented for readers to tryout and figure stuff out for themselves.BOOK DESIGN BY RACHEL GRIFFIN OF EARNEST STUDIO.
