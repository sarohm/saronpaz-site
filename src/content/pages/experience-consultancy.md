Experience have become the core value of today’s economy. Any business needs to pay special attention to the experience it provides both to it’s customers and employees.

Though much has been discovered and implemented within the virtual realm experience the area of sensual, spacial, social and physical interaction experience is yet to be explored.

We offer a peek into the exciting possibilities experience design can offer through consulting individuals and businesses in the ways meaningful experience can do them good.

We have experience in a large variety of areas, opportunities and challenges : retail, education, museums, academy, high tech, public sphere and many more.

We Welcome inspiring talks and visionary ideas and fruitful collaborations.
